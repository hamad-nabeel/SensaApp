Terms and Conditions

Use of SensaApp

SensaApp provides community-submitted sensory information about campus locations. Reports may be delayed, incomplete, or inaccurate, and should be used as general guidance only.

Accounts

You are responsible for keeping your account details secure and for providing accurate registration information. Do not share your password or use another person's account.

Update Requests and Reports

Update requests help ambassadors identify locations that may need newer sensory reports. Submitted reports and requests should be respectful, relevant, and based on genuine campus use.

No Emergency or Accessibility Guarantee

SensaApp is not an emergency, safety, medical, or official accessibility service. For urgent support, safety concerns, or formal accessibility accommodations, contact the appropriate university service.

Data Use

SensaApp stores account information and app activity needed to operate the service, including update requests and submitted reports. By creating an account, you agree that this information may be used to provide and improve the app.

Campuses

About SensaApp

SensaApp helps students find campus spaces that match their sensory needs by showing recent reports on noise, crowding, lighting, and temperature. If a location needs fresher information, anyone can request an update for an ambassador to review.
